Par

Timing

The delay is chosen at random between min and max before each start beep. Set both equal for a fixed delay. For reps after the first, the wait after the previous par beep is the rest plus the delay. Par is measured from the start of the start beep.

Call outs

One call out is picked at random for each rep, so the same one can repeat. "Before" announces it shortly after the rep becomes ready, ahead of the random delay; keep the start delay minimum at 1 s or more so it finishes before the beep. Test beeps also plays a sample call out.

Sound

Volume here is the beep level inside the app. The phone's volume still applies.
